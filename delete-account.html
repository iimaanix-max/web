Account & Personal Data Deletion Request

Google Play Data Safety & DPDP Act 2023 Compliant Erasure Portal

🔒 Privacy Commitment: Core tasbih counters, offline Quran reading markers, and daily Athkar are stored 100% locally on your phone. We never sell user data. This page allows you to permanently erase any online community or campaign contest records.

1. Instant On-Device Deletion (Offline Data)

If you only used the offline features (Tasbih counter, Quran, Prayers):

  1. Open Android Settings on your phone.
  2. Go to Apps (or Manage Apps) ➔ Digital Tasbih.
  3. Tap Storage & cache ➔ Clear Storage (Clear Data).

This permanently wipes all local count logs, badge progression, and saved prayer locations from your phone.

2. Request Cloud & Contest Record Deletion

If you registered for the Mega Umrah Contest or joined Live Dhikr Rooms with an email, phone number, or Google account, send a deletion request using the form below. We will review your request and contact you if we need more information.

⚠️ What Will Be Erased by Imaanix:
  • Your mobile phone number from contest databases.
  • Your registered email address, full name, and city.
  • Your Firebase Authentication account and Google Sign-In links.
  • Cloudflare D1 login verification records and OTP tables.
  • Your live room participation logs and Umrah ticket entries.
  • Your device's Firebase Analytics App Instance ID (instantly reset).

📌 Scope of Deletion: This request erases all personal data held and processed directly on Imaanix servers.

3. How to Delete Your Google Advertising ID (AdMob Tracker)

Third-party advertising identifiers are managed directly by Google Play Services, not stored on Imaanix servers. You can permanently delete or reset your advertising identifier from your device in 1 tap:

  1. Open your phone's Android Settings.
  2. Scroll down and tap Google (or Google Services).
  3. Tap on Ads (or Privacy • Ads).
  4. Tap Delete Advertising ID (or Reset Advertising ID) and confirm.

Once deleted, Google permanently removes your device's persistent advertising ID, preventing ad networks from building any cross-app advertising profile for your phone.

Your email app should open with a prepared deletion request. The request is sent only after you review and send that email. If no email app opens, contact support@imaanix.in directly.
